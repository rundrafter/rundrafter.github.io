RunDrafter

Tell us the race. We'll draft the training.

RunDrafter turns a goal race and the week you actually have into a periodised training plan - phase lengths, weekly volume, and session-by-session paces, grounded in established training methodology and written up by an AI model. Fill in the form below and download the intake.json it produces; email it in and a person runs the pipeline and replies with your plan - this is not instant, so expect a wait of a few days, not a download.

B races

Other events

Optional - one-off events you'll take part in but not race, e.g. a charity fun run or a club social. No de-load is inserted; the plan folds the event into that day's easy or long run.

So a real commitment on the calendar doesn't collide with a prescribed hard session.

Schedule

Everything below is optional. Leave a cell alone and RunDrafter decides for you - override only what you have a genuine preference about.

Weekly availability

So the plan fits the week you actually have, instead of one you'd need to adapt yourself to. Each half of each day is available by default; mark it unavailable, or give it a standing session (a club night, a parkrun, a long run, a gym session) it should be built around.

Notes

Not everything fits a structured field above - an old injury that's now resolved but still shapes preference, a race history detail, a scheduling quirk the grid can't express. For example: "I get shin pain if I ramp weekly distance too quickly" or "I'd rather do my long run on Sunday even in weeks where Saturday looks free." This text is passed to the plan's AI-written design brief as your own words - it can influence phrasing and emphasis, but not override any deterministic rule like phase lengths, volume, or taper.
